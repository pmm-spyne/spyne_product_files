Shooting a Vehicle 2 use cases ▾
Shoot a vehicle from scratch using the app
OB Internal + External Calls
How it works
1Open app, log in, tap Start Shoot, scan the VIN barcode
2Select vehicle body type (sedan, SUV, pickup) so the white overlay stencil matches the car shape
3Hold phone at chest height, 6-10 ft from car. Green screen = correct angle. Red = tilt fix needed
4Walk around in 30 to 90 seconds for the 360 spin video. Do not go under 30 sec
5App shows guided stencils for each exterior angle. Capture all, then move inside for dashboard, steering, odometer
6Tap Process. Spyne AI processes in background. Status shows in Processed / Processing / Failed dashboard
Shoot when there is less direct sunlight to avoid body panel reflections affecting background removal.

First shoot practice: scan VIN, select body type, use stencil overlay
OB Internal + External Calls
How it works
1Tap Start Shoot. The in-app VIN scanner opens. Scan the barcode on the driver side door jamb to auto-populate the VIN
2Verify the VIN number on screen. Proceed to vehicle type selection: Sedan, SUV, Hatchback, Pickup Truck, Van
3The stencil overlay on your camera screen changes shape to match the selected body type. Align the car body inside the stencil before shooting each angle
4App guides you through each required angle sequentially. You cannot skip required angles -- the app will not move forward until that angle is captured
If the VIN barcode is damaged or unreadable, type the VIN manually into the scan screen. Do not select the wrong body type -- the stencil shape affects alignment quality for all angles.

QC and Status 1 use case ▾
Read QC status: what "In Review", "Failed", and "Incomplete" actually mean
OB + CS Internal + External Calls
Status definitions
1In Review = images are processed and currently being checked by Spyne QC team. Do not reshoot
2Failed = QC rejected the output. Can be due to bad input angle, crop, or AI error. Raise a concern to CSM or raise a remark from console
3Incomplete = shoot was done but Process was never tapped. Resume from app, no new shoot needed
4360 Failed = only the spin failed, not the images. Do not reshoot the full car. Raise a remark and QC team fixes it
QC SLA is 24 hours. If a 360 fails, raise a remark within 4 hours if you want to avoid going back to the lot.

Publishing Flow 4 use cases ▾
Understand the full end-to-end publishing flow (app to website)
OB Internal Only Calls
How it works
1Shoot or upload images via app or console. Spyne processes and backgrounds them
2QC: AI processes first. Manual QC team reviews and marks as Assured. SLA: 3-6 hours typical
3Images: sent to your DMS/IMS (HomeNet, vAuto, WinSolutions etc) via integration. IMS pushes to website and marketplaces (CarGurus, AutoTrader etc). Timeline: 12-14 hours total from shoot to website
4360 spin and video: sent directly to website provider via a script (not through IMS). Appear on VDP only, not on listing pages
5Track status in console: In Review > Assured > Published. Green checkmark on a VIN = sent to DMS successfully
If images show as Published in Spyne but not live on website, the DMS has received them but not yet refreshed. Escalate to the DMS provider, not Spyne. Spyne's job ends at the DMS delivery.

What happens to a VIN when it gets sold (draft mode and re-trade)
CS Internal Only Calls
How it works
1When a vehicle is marked sold in your DMS, Spyne stops receiving its feed. Images remain stored in console but stop publishing
2If the same VIN re-enters inventory (trade-in comes back), and the DMS sends it with zero images: Spyne deletes the old images and puts the VIN in Draft mode
3Draft mode = not live on website. You need to either reshoot via the app or upload new images via console to reactivate it
4If using Smart Match / VIN Cloning: Spyne checks if a previous match exists and can auto-apply old media to the re-entered VIN without reshoot

How images reach CarGurus, AutoTrader, and other marketplaces
Sales + OB Internal Only Calls
How it works
1Images: Spyne sends to your IMS/DMS (vAuto, HomeNet, WinSolutions). Your IMS syndicates them to marketplaces as part of their normal feed
2360 spin and video: Spyne sends a script directly to your website provider. These appear only on the VDP -- not on listing pages or marketplace thumbnails
3Spyne does not currently send scripts directly to CarGurus or AutoTrader. Any marketplace visibility for 360/video depends on those platforms supporting embedded iframes (CarGurus integration is in progress)
4For new cars without photos yet: VIN Cloning finds a match in existing inventory (same make/model/trim/color) and publishes instantly while the physical car is being photographed
This is a common confusion point in sales demos. Be precise: Spyne controls the VDP experience. Marketplace thumbnails are controlled by the IMS syndication, not Spyne directly.

IMS and DMS integration: what access Spyne needs and how to collect it
OB Internal Only Calls
How it works
1Spyne integrates with IMS (vAuto, HomeNet, WinSolutions, DealerCenter, Dealer Socket, Pivoto) for image import and export. Confirm the dealer's IMS on the first call
